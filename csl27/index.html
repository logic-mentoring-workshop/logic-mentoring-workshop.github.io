15th Logic Mentoring Workshop

Brighton, UK

Co-located with Computer Science Logic (CSL) 2027

Overview

The Logic Mentoring Workshop (LMW) introduces young researchers to the technical and practical aspects of a career in logic research. It is targeted at students, from senior undergraduates to graduates, and will include talks and panel sessions from leaders in the subject.
